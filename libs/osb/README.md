# One Step Beyond (osb)

One Step Beyond (OSB) is an English-language fantasy LPMud descended from Nightfall and founded in May 1997 by Lars Düning (`Mateese`) and team, preserved here from the 2003-02-18 public release (`osb-lib-030218.tar.gz`). After greeting players with the classic castle `WELCOME` art and the *Entering One Step Beyond* startup chamber, characters step ashore at the docks of Silvere harbour to explore the northern defense tower, royal shipyard, harbour crossing and cobbled streets, *The Seaman's Rest* tavern (with menu and sailors' shanty), and the Harbourmaster's office.
