# Heaven7 (v2.07)

Heaven7 v2.07 (1994), a COMPAT-mode LPMud 2.4.5-descended mudlib centered on the walled city of Tempus — featuring city guards, bulletin boards, banks, pubs, crafted arms and alchemy shops, multi-class skill spheres (fighter, thief, mage, and cleric), and documented builder examples.
