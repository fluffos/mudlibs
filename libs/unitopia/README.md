# UNItopia (unitopia)

UNItopia is a German-language university LPMud with roots at the University of Stuttgart, uniting a virtual campus with the fantasy world of *Magyra*. Visitors arrive in the Cathedral of Tadmor (*Kathedrale*) and can explore the base of the north bell tower, the Rathaus steps and foyer signpost, the *Gang der Gilden* guild corridor, the player council wing, the *Halle der Reinkarnation*, and *Das Forum*.
