ONI

Oni are the traditional horned demons' of Japanese myth. Similar to Western trolls and ogres, Oni are incarnate
spirits of anger who appear as powerful, hulking monsters in garish shades of blue, red , pink or green. Their
major distinguishing feature is the pair of large horns on thier heads, although they may also have animal
attributes, or monstrous characteristics such as three eyes or extra fingers and toes. A vengeful person who dies
under very tragic circumstances may return as an Oni in order to avenge the death of loved ones.
