# SilberLand (silberland)

SilberLand is a German-language fantasy LPMud descended from MorgenGrauen. Players arrive at the Adventurers' Guild (*Die beruehmte Abenteurergilde*) beneath its oak-beamed loft (*Das juengste Geruecht*) and step out onto the cobblestones of Midas, exploring its stone chapel, the tavern *Zur roten Nase* and upstairs dining hall, the general store, postal center, western city gate, and cathedral-side alleys.
